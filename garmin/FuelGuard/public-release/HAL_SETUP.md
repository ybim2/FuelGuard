# Fuel Guard Garmin public-beta setup for Hal

Use this guide after Garmin approves both public listings.

## Install

1. Open the **Connect IQ Store** mobile app.
2. Search for **Fuel Guard**.
3. Install **Fuel Guard Quick Log**.
4. Install **Fuel Guard Activity Logger**.

## Connect your Fuel Guard account

Repeat for each app:

1. On the watch, choose **Connect Fuel Guard**.
2. Open the approval notification in the Connect IQ Store app on your phone.
3. Sign in to Fuel Guard and approve the connection.
4. Wait for the watch to display **Connected**.

## Everyday logging

Open **Fuel Guard Quick Log**, choose **Fuel**, **Hydrate**, **Sleepy** or the Training Mode action, then press START. The Training action starts or ends the same Fuel Guard Training Mode used by the phone app.

## During training

1. Add **Fuel Guard Activity Logger** as a Data Field in the Garmin activity profile you want to use.
2. On a Forerunner 255, disable Auto Lap first.
3. Start the activity.
4. Press LAP once when you want to record Fuel. The lap remains a normal Garmin lap too.
5. Continue training normally.

Activity Logger records Fuel only; it does not currently have a separate Hydration button. On the Forerunner 255, structured-workout or other automatic lap events can also be interpreted as Fuel events, so use a simple activity profile with Auto Lap disabled during the beta.
